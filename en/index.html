View on App Store Privacy details
Does AI save transactions automatically?

No. AI helps create reviewable transaction drafts. You can still adjust the amount, date, category, and note before saving.

Does MyFinance Go give financial advice?

No. The app is a personal finance record-keeping tool. It does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advisory services.

Who handles payments and subscriptions?

Subscriptions, in-app purchases, purchase restoration, and refund flows are handled according to Apple App Store / StoreKit rules.

Can I delete my account or data?

Yes. You can follow the account deletion flow in the app, or contact support if you need help with privacy-related requests.

Do you sell financial data or use it for ad tracking?

No. MyFinance Go does not sell personal financial records, and data use is explained in the privacy policy.

Why does the English name include Go?

The App Store and English market already include similar names, so the English listing uses MyFinance Go. The product remains MyFinance in Chinese and Japanese contexts.
