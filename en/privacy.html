1. Service Description

The App provides personal finance management and expense tracking services, including but not limited to:

  • Personal transaction records
  • Category and ledger management

  • AI-assisted transaction parsing

The App is a personal finance management tool. It does not provide investment advice, financial services, or financial consulting services.
