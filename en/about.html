About My Journey

Hello, I am the creator of MyFinanceApp. I have always been drawn to science, emerging technology, and the feeling of learning something new that changes how everyday life can work.

Travel is another part of that curiosity. When I visit a new place, I want the practical things, including money records, to stay simple enough that they do not interrupt the experience.

Why I Built MyFinanceApp

When you are enjoying coffee by the Seine or walking through old streets in Kyoto, the last thing you want is a complicated expense form.

I built MyFinanceApp because many finance apps feel either too heavy for quick daily use or too limited when you need structure. The goal is to combine modern app design, AI-assisted entry, and cloud sync into a finance tool that feels clear, calm, and practical.

Product Principles

Fast to record

Transaction entry should stay lightweight, especially when you are busy or traveling.

Easy to trust

Financial records need clear structure, readable reports, and privacy-aware handling.

Ready to grow

Multiple ledgers and custom categories help the app fit different goals over time.
