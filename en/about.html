About MyFinance Go

A finance tool for real life, not just a prettier spreadsheet.

MyFinance Go starts from a simple idea: tracking should be fast, but your data should stay under your control. AI can help, but you should still review before saving. Reports should be readable, and they should connect back to budgets, assets, and everyday choices.

Clarity matters more than spectacle

A finance app may be opened every day, so the interface needs to be stable, predictable, and easy to scan. More features only help when the next step is obvious.

AI remains assistive

AI can turn natural language into a draft, but it should not silently make the final decision. MyFinance Go keeps review and correction in the flow.

Data handling should be understandable

Sign-in, sync, AI parsing, purchases, and account deletion all involve trust, so the app and website provide clear privacy and support entry points.

Technical foundation

Native iOS experience for everyday finance workflows.

MyFinance Go uses SwiftUI for the interface, SwiftData for local records, and Firebase plus StoreKit for sync, AI features, memberships, and purchase entitlements.

SwiftUI + SwiftData

Keeps tracking, reports, settings, and finance management feeling native on iOS.

Firebase Sync

Supports sign-in, cloud sync, shared ledgers, Functions, and App Check.

AI Assistance

Used for transaction text parsing and report summaries, with review before saving.

StoreKit 2

Subscriptions and one-time modules are handled through Apple App Store flows.
