Core Features

Personal finance tools for
clearer everyday decisions

MyFinanceApp combines quick entry, structured ledgers, secure sync, and readable reports so you can spend less time organizing records and more time understanding them.

AI-assisted transaction entry

Turn natural text into structured records

Instead of manually choosing every field, enter a short phrase such as "ramen in Shinjuku, 1,200 yen today." The AI-assisted parser helps identify the amount, date, category, and note so the record is easier to complete.

"45 euros for souvenirs at the Louvre"
Shopping Today
EUR 45.00
Louvre souvenirs

Secure cloud sync

Firebase account-based synchronization

Keep your records available when you change devices or use more than one device. Sign in securely and let your ledgers travel with you.

Multiple ledgers

Separate records for different goals

Create dedicated ledgers for daily spending, travel plans, shared expenses, or savings goals so each area stays organized.

Visual reports

Readable statistics and spending trends

Review spending by category, period, and ledger with charts that make your financial patterns easier to understand.

Custom categories

Adapt your category structure to how you actually spend, from recurring household costs to one-time travel categories.

Privacy-aware design

Your records are used to provide the app experience. They are not sold or provided to third parties for marketing purposes.

A calmer way to keep financial records

Start with simple records, then use ledgers, categories, sync, and reports as your needs grow.
